# Disclaimer

Underhood performs static analysis only. A finding is evidence of a declaration or bundled string, not a verdict of safety, maliciousness, runtime behavior, permission grants or network traffic. Missing findings do not prove that a capability is absent.

Signatures are parsed, not cryptographically verified. Gatekeeper and notarization are not assessed. Apps may behave differently from their declarations, and dynamically constructed behavior may be invisible.
